# Island — Layer 4: The Village (4a) and Life Goes On (4b)

Builds on Layers 1–3 and the crew/menus round. The same rules apply:
- **Mouse only, no learning curve.** Systems run by themselves; you steer.
- **No numbers on screen.** State shows as words, piles and animation.
- **The camp decides where; you decide what.** Every place is chosen sensibly.
- **One difficulty.** Nights are never skipped.

The layer is split so each half is playable on its own: **4a, The village**, then **4b, Life goes on**.

User decisions (2026-10-18):
- The village era happens **both** ways: it becomes possible on its own, then you name it at a fire ceremony.
- Discoveries are **clue-driven**.
- All four discoveries are in: pottery, farming, canoe, herbal medicine.
- A lost challenge means **you keep playing as the winner**.
- The ending is decided later.
- Traditions, babies/families, tamed animals (dog, goats, chickens, parrot) and storm season are all in.
- Other people on the island: you leaned toward early hints but wanted to talk it over after this layer, so Layer 4 adds none.
- The volcano **rumbles** in Layer 4.
- Kids grow up in **20–30 days**.
- The map stays open on click; add a camp overview page and a pause menu.
- Tower and traps: **the tower sees, the traps catch**.
- Babies come from **couples and new love**.
- Challenges are a **showdown at the fire**.
- **You can paddle the canoe** to the reef and islets.

## 4a — The village

### The Village era
- **Ready when the camp is settled.** All of these must be true for 2 dawns in a row:
  - everyone has a roof;
  - food is not Low or Out at dawn;
  - nobody is very sick;
  - the cat is not stalking (it's dead or hasn't shown up).
- When it's ready, someone well-liked comes to you: *"We're not a camp anymore. We're a village. Let's make it official tonight."* You answer **TONIGHT** or **NOT YET**. Not answering counts as tonight.
- **Naming ceremony** at dusk:
  - Everyone gathers round the fire.
  - The name box appears with suggestions from people: the leader's name + "'s Landing", the name of someone who died, "Second Chance", "Flight [n]", the biggest landmark.
  - Then the title card **THE VILLAGE OF …**, a feast (everyone happy, a cheer, food used), a journal line and the founding day saved.
- The Village era unlocks:
  - the lookout tower;
  - spike traps;
  - babies (4b);
  - traditions (4b);
  - leadership challenges (4b);
  - more frequent discovery clues.

### Discoveries (clue-driven)
Four of them. Each follows the same path:
1. **Clue.** Something happens in the world and a fitting person notices it. The clue gets a journal line.
2. **Pitch.** That person asks you through the normal request bubble: *"The clay by the river… I think I could make pots."* Answering **TRY IT** starts it. **NOT NOW** shelves it; the idea comes back in a few days.
3. **Experimenting.** A new automatic job, **Experimenting**, gets one person (the best fit), and a second if things are slow.
   - Progress shows in words: *Trying → Getting closer → Almost*.
   - Visible failures happen along the way, each with a toast and a journal line: a cracked pot by the fire, a wilted plot, a raft that sinks with someone splashing back to shore, a bitter leaf making someone sick for a moment.
4. **Discovered.** A title card **DISCOVERY: POTTERY**, a cheer, the journal, and it unlocks things.

| Discovery | Clue | Who notices | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pottery | Water carriers notice clay at the river bank (after some days of the Water job) | cook, curious, hardworking | **Kiln** (built by the fire). With it: the evening meal stretches further (stews), rain catchers fill twice as fast, and spoilage drops (storage pots). |
| Farming | Fruit dropped by the food pile sprouts | hardworking, kind, any gatherer | **Garden** plots. **Farming** job: tend daily; a harvest every 3 days of steady fruit, more in rain, less in a dry spell. |
| Canoe | A fisher or wood cutter sees a log floating offshore | fisher, builder, curious | A **canoe** beached on the shore. Fishing brings more back; you can paddle it (below). |
| Herbal medicine | A sick person chews a jungle leaf and feels better, or the medic finds plants while out | medic, kind | An **Herbs** job: gathering in the jungle. With herbs in stock, the sick get better more often, deaths from sickness halve, and infected wounds become rarer. |

- The JOBS tab gets a **DISCOVERIES** section: discovered ones (with what they do), the one in progress (word stage), and shelved ideas.

### The canoe (you can use it)
- Once built, the canoe sits on the beach (3D).
- **Click open water** near the coast to go there by canoe. You walk to the canoe first; up to three of your crew climb in and the rest wait on the beach.
- Paddling uses a water path around the island. People sit in the canoe and paddle.
- **Click land** while paddling to land at the nearest beach to that spot. The canoe stays beached there until you take it again.
- **Islets and reef** (the small islands offshore) each hold something you can only reach by canoe:
  - a seabird colony (eggs: food);
  - giant clams on the reef (food);
  - a castaway's old camp (story, a tool);
  - a sealed crate from the plane's cargo (scrap and a knife).
- Each one is a first-sighting moment you can name. Islets show on the map once seen.

### Lookout tower
- A Village-era build; the camp puts it on high ground near camp. Proper 3D: posts, platform, ladder and roof.
- **Lookout job (day):** someone climbs up and watches.
  - It slowly reveals places within a large radius of the tower (known on the map, with a first-sighting moment).
  - It warns of the cat approaching at dusk (people get inside the fence).
  - It warns a day ahead of storms (*"Storm clouds to the east. Tomorrow."*).

### Spike traps
- A Village-era build. The camp places a few pits along animal trails at the jungle edge, 30–45 steps from the fire.
- The **Checking snares** job also checks traps. Some mornings a trap has caught a boar (meat and a hide).
- **The cat:** if it prowls near a trap, there's a chance it's wounded (*"It's limping."*). Hunting it gets easier and it stays away a few nights.
- **Accidents:** anyone walking past a trap at night (hunters, gatherers, your crew) has a small chance to step in it and get hurt. Clumsy people are more at risk.

### Storm season
- Roughly every 8–10 days a big storm builds.
- **Warning:** the tower warns a day ahead. Without a tower, people notice signs that morning (*"The birds are gone. Something's coming."*).
- The morning plan offers **PREPARE FOR THE STORM**. If chosen:
  - builders shore up shaky buildings (no longer shaky);
  - food goes into storage (no storm loss);
  - people stay close to camp.
- **The storm itself:** forced storm weather for half a day.
  - Shaky buildings collapse (existing rule).
  - Unprepared, there's a chance a sound building is damaged (back to "Almost done").
  - The food pile loses some if there's no storage.
  - Afterwards there's a recap line, and trust goes up if the camp came through well.

### Volcano rumbles
- From the Village era, the volcano stirs:
  - Now and then a tremor shakes the camera, with a toast (*"The ground shook."*) and startled people.
  - A thin smoke plume rises from the crater and thickens slowly.
  - People talk about it at the fire.
  - A rumor marker appears: *"Smoke from the mountain."*
- No eruption in Layer 4; it's set up for later.

### Menus
- **The map stays open** when clicked: the camera moves there and the map stays up.
- **Camp overview page.** A new **CAMP** button next to PEOPLE and ISLAND (the zoom bar's camera button becomes a house icon). On one page, all in words:
  - the village name and founding day;
  - buildings (built / going up / shaky);
  - where everyone is (working, with you, scouting, sick, resting);
  - the stockpile (food, water, materials, tools);
  - discoveries;
  - traditions (4b);
  - coming problems (storm warning, goals/problems).
- **Pause menu.** Esc (when nothing else is open) pauses the game, dims the world and shows **RESUME / SAVE / HELP / SETTINGS / QUIT TO TITLE**.

## 4b — Life goes on

### Couples and new love
- Couples who arrived together count from the start.
- **New love:** two unattached adults whose bond stays very high for a few days become a couple. There's a toast (*"Ada and Joel are together now."*), a journal line, they sit together at the fire and share a hut when there's one.
- Breakups are not in Layer 4.

### Babies and kids
- **Expecting:** in the Village era, a couple with a hut, steady food, nobody very sick and no baby in the last 12 days has a small chance each dawn to be *expecting* (toast and journal).
- **Birth:** 6 days later, a baby is born at dawn. Everyone is happy and you name the baby, with suggestions such as the names of the dead or the parents' family names.
- **Growing up:** the baby becomes a new survivor who grows up over **25 days**:
  - **Baby** (days 0–5): stays at the parents' hut, carried around camp.
  - **Kid** (days 5–25): a small sprite. Plays near camp, tags along with a parent at work and helps a little.
  - **Teen** (day 25+): picks up a job like anyone else.
- **What they inherit:** a trait from each parent. They grow up good at the job of the parent who spent most time with them (Handy at the start). Their look mixes the parents'.
- Kids are never in crews or on night watch, and never get sick from hunger first (adults feed them first).

### Tamed animals
- **Wild dog:**
  - A scrawny dog starts scavenging at the camp edge at dusk.
  - A kind person asks to feed it. Over three evenings it comes closer, then you name it.
  - The camp dog follows you on trips, sleeps by the fire, barks at the cat at night (the cat backs off like it does from a torch) and sniffs out small finds more often.
- **Goats:**
  - Someone exploring the hills spots wild goats and asks to catch some. A **goat pen** is built.
  - Hunters bring two goats home. The pen gives milk daily and the goats slowly multiply.
  - If food runs out, one is butchered (meat).
- **Chickens:**
  - Jungle fowl are spotted, and a **coop** is built.
  - Eggs every morning.
- **Parrot:**
  - One day a parrot lands at camp and a curious or kind person befriends it.
  - It perches by the fire and repeats things people say a lot, including the names of the dead.
- All animals are pixel sprites with walk and idle frames, and they're saved.

### Traditions
- Born from what happened in your game. Someone proposes one through a request (yes makes it a tradition, no lets it go):
  - **Night of the Dead:** after the first death. Every 10 days at dusk, people gather at the graves with torches and say the names.
  - **[Cat]'s Night:** after the cat is killed. A feast with the legend retold and dancing.
  - **Landing Day:** every 10 days counted from the crash. Everyone walks to the wreck, then shares food.
  - **Founding Day:** every 10 days from the village founding. A feast, and the name is said aloud.
  - **First Harvest:** after the first garden harvest. Everyone eats fruit together and happy moods last long.
- During one there's a title card, gathering and poses, mood and trust boosts, and a journal line. They're listed on the camp overview.

### Leadership challenges
- **When:** average trust in you sits at Unsure or below for 2 dawns, or right after a death the camp blames on neglect. At most once every 8 days, and not before the Village era.
- **Who:** the most liked person who trusts you least.
- **Showdown at the fire (dusk):**
  - Everyone gathers. The challenger makes their case, using real things from your game: deaths, hunger, a turned-down good idea.
  - You pick one of three replies:
    - **Admit mistakes:** works on kind, honest and calm people.
    - **Remind them what you've done:** strong if you have finds, builds, rescues and a cat legend.
    - **Push back:** hotheads and the brave respect it; it backfires on others.
  - People then walk to stand behind one side or the other. Each choice is driven by trust in you, their bond with the challenger and how your reply landed.
- **You win:** the challenger sulks, everyone else's trust goes up a little, and a journal line is written.
- **You lose:** the challenger becomes the leader and **you play on as them**. You get a title card *"[Name] leads now"*. Trust is recalculated from everyone's bond with the new leader. The old leader is now a regular survivor who holds a grudge and may challenge back later.

### Saving
Everything new is saved:
- village name and day;
- discoveries (state and stage);
- clues;
- canoe position;
- tower and traps;
- storm timer;
- volcano level;
- couples and pregnancies;
- born people (their full persona);
- animals;
- traditions;
- the challenge timer.

Old saves still load: relationships are remapped and the new people list is optional.
